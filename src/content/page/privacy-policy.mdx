      # The Lippincott Team Privacy Policy
      
      **Effective Date:** 01/01/2026
      
      **1\. Information We Collect** We collect personal information necessary to facilitate real estate transactions. This includes:
      
      - **Contact Information:** Name, email address, phone number, and physical address.
      - **Financial Information:** Pre-approval letters, mortgage details, credit readiness, and proof of funds.
      - **Property Data:** Transaction history, property preferences, and showing feedback.
      
      **2\. How We Use Your Information** We use the collected information exclusively to:
      
      - Provide real estate brokerage services (buying, selling, or leasing properties).
      - Communicate administrative notices, property listings, and transaction updates.
      - Comply with local, state, and federal legal obligations.
      
      **3\. Information Sharing and Disclosure** We do not sell personal data to third parties. We share information strictly with entities required to complete a real estate transaction. These third parties include:
      
      - Title companies, escrow agents, and closing attorneys.
      - Mortgage lenders and financial institutions.
      - Home inspectors, appraisers, and relevant contractors.
      - Multiple Listing Services (MLS) as required by industry rules.
      
      **4\. Data Security** We implement standard administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.
      
      **5\. Your Privacy Rights** Depending on your legal jurisdiction, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. To submit a data request, contact us at:
