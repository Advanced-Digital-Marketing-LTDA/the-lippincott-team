  - title: Waller ISD, **answered.**
    jsonld: true
    items:
      - question: Is Waller ISD a good school district?
        answer: |
          It is a B-graded, fast-improving district: 3.86 out of 5 on Niche, ranked #437 in Texas, with enrollment up 65.4% since 2016. Ratings trail A-graded Tomball and Cy-Fair today, and growth is funding new campuses every year.
      - question: What cities does Waller ISD serve?
        answer: |
          The district covers 328 square miles of Waller and northwest Harris counties, serving Waller, Hockley and Prairie View plus surrounding unincorporated communities.
      - question: How much is a home in Waller ISD in 2026?
        answer: |
          HAR's September 2026 single-family active-listing medians were [$329,925 in Waller](https://www.har.com/waller/realestate/pricetrends) and [$334,995 in Hockley](https://www.har.com/hockley/realestate/pricetrends). Prairie View's [single-family active-listing median was $244,450 in September 2026](https://www.har.com/prairieview/realestate/pricetrends). New construction in communities like Jubilee starts in the low $300s.
      - question: Is the Waller area a buyer's market in 2026?
        answer: |
          Realtor.com's June 2026 snapshot reported homes selling at 97% of asking. HAR's [September 2026 single-family data](https://www.har.com/waller/realestate/pricetrends) shows 493 active listings, up 38.48% year over year, with 50 median active-listing days. These figures describe Waller, not every neighborhood or school attendance zone.
      - question: How does Waller ISD compare to Tomball ISD?
        answer: |
          Tomball ISD is A+-graded and #1 in Harris County on Niche; Waller ISD is B-graded but about $70,075 cheaper at the city median ($329,925 vs $400,000, HAR single-family listings, September 2026) with more land per dollar.
      - question: What high school serves Waller ISD?
        answer: |
          Waller High School, the district's only high school: 2,846 students, a 94% graduation rate, ranked #917 in Texas (U.S. News).
      - question: How far is Waller from downtown Houston?
        answer: |
          About 44 miles via US-290 (Travelmath). Hockley, at the district's southeast edge near the Grand Parkway, trims ten minutes or more off that drive.
      - question: What new communities are coming to Waller ISD?
        answer: |
          Jubilee (1,622 acres, wellness-focused, from the low $300s), Cypress Green and Dellrose lead the Hockley corridor, with thousands more homes planned across the district (Wolff Companies, 2025).
