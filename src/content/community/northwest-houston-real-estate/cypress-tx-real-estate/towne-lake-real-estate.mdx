  - title: Towne Lake, **answered.**
    jsonld: true
    items:
      - question: Is Towne Lake a good place to live?
        answer: |
          Yes if you will use the water: a 300-acre boatable lake, The Boardwalk's waterfront dining, a resident waterpark and on-site Cy-Fair ISD schools. The honest trade-offs are 3.3-3.7% property tax rates and higher HOA dues.
      - question: How much is a home in Towne Lake in 2026?
        answer: |
          The median listing price was $740,000 in June 2026 and the median sold price $536,575 (Realtor.com). Interior sections close well under the waterfront ask; custom estates in Water's Edge and Great Oaks Estates run above $1M.
      - question: Is Towne Lake a buyer's market in 2026?
        answer: |
          Realtor.com classifies it as one in June 2026, with homes selling at 98% of asking. HAR listed 91 homes for sale on October 3, 2026; the June sale-to-list figure is a separate historical measure.
      - question: How long does it take to sell a home in Towne Lake?
        answer: |
          The median was 34 days on market in June 2026 (Realtor.com). Accurate pricing against your section's comparable sales is the deciding factor.
      - question: What schools serve Towne Lake?
        answer: |
          Cypress-Fairbanks ISD, rated A and ranked #2 in Harris County (Niche, 2026). On-site schools are Rennell Elementary, Postma Elementary and Anthony Middle School (8/10, GreatSchools); the feeder high school is Cypress Ranch, ranked #192 in Texas (U.S. News).
      - question: What are the property taxes in Towne Lake?
        answer: |
          The total rate runs 3.3% to 3.7% before exemptions, depending on which MUD your section sits in (Houston Properties, 2026). Verify the MUD rate per address; the homestead exemption lowers the bill.
      - question: How far is Towne Lake from downtown Houston?
        answer: |
          About 25 to 30 miles northwest via US-290, typically 40 to 60 minutes in traffic. The Energy Corridor is closer at 25 to 35 minutes, and IAH airport runs 35 to 45 minutes via the Grand Parkway.
      - question: Does Towne Lake flood?
        answer: |
          The community was engineered with the lake, channels and detention systems as its stormwater infrastructure, but flood risk is address-specific anywhere in Harris County. Check flood history and elevation per home before buying; we do this for every client.
