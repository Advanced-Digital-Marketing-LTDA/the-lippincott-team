      - question: Is Bridgeland a good place to live?
        answer: |
          Yes for most buyers who want newer construction and outdoor amenities: 3,000 acres of lakes, trails and parks, mostly Cy-Fair ISD schools (the newest western villages are Waller ISD), and the district's top-ranked high school. The honest trade-offs are the MUD tax rate and peak-hour commutes.
      - question: How much is a home in Bridgeland in 2026?
        answer: |
          HAR does not publish a dated Bridgeland-wide median; Cypress citywide single-family listings had a $450,000 median in September 2026 (HAR). Village medians run from $444,950 in Parkland Village to $584,500 in Lakeland Village — its gated Water Haven waterfront section runs about $619,000 — and new construction in Bridgeland Central, Creekland and Prairieland starts from the mid-$300s (Bridgeland.com).
      - question: Is Bridgeland a buyer's market in 2026?
        answer: |
          It leans that way. HAR's Cypress single-family inventory rose 4.71% year over year in September 2026, with a median 42 days on market, and Realtor.com's June 2026 snapshot had Cypress homes selling at about 98% of asking.
      - question: How long does it take to sell a home in Bridgeland?
        answer: |
          Cypress single-family listings had a median 42 days on market in September 2026 (HAR). In Bridgeland you are often competing with builder inventory, so pricing against new-construction incentives matters.
      - question: What school district serves Bridgeland?
        answer: |
          Two. Most of the community is Cypress-Fairbanks ISD, rated A and ranked #2 in Harris County (Niche, 2026), with Bridgeland High School at #126 in Texas and #1 in the district, with a 97% graduation rate (U.S. News, 2026). The newer western villages — Prairieland and Creekland — are zoned to Waller ISD, which opened McReavy Elementary inside Prairieland in August 2024; those addresses currently feed Waller Junior High and Waller High School. Zoning is village-specific, and we verify it for every address.
      - question: How far is Bridgeland from downtown Houston?
        answer: |
          About 28 miles northwest via US-290, with the Grand Parkway at the community's edge. Peak commutes run 25 to 40 minutes to the Energy Corridor and 45 to 60 minutes downtown (Houston Properties).
      - question: Does Bridgeland flood?
        answer: |
          Its stormwater system meets or exceeds Harris County requirements, including the stricter post-Harvey regulations, and performed through Harvey per a ULI case study. Check flood history per address before buying; we do this for every client.
      - question: What are property taxes like in Bridgeland?
        answer: |
          Combined rates run about 3.0% to 3.5% depending on the village MUD (Houston Properties), higher than established areas because the MUD funds the community's infrastructure. Rates typically step down as build-out completes.
