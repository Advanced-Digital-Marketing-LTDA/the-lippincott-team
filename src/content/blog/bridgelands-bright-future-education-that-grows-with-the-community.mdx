It’s no secret that Cypress, Texas is booming. Families are arriving in droves, and [Bridgeland](/northwest-houston-real-estate/cypress-tx-real-estate/bridgeland-real-estate/) continues to be one of the top choices for those looking to put down roots. With that growth comes one of the most important questions homebuyers ask: what are the schools like?

In Bridgeland, education is thriving. From award-winning public schools to standout private and charter institutions, the community is raising the bar for academic excellence. And it’s just getting started.

### A New Chapter for Cypress Christian School
Cypress Christian School has been a cornerstone of the Cypress area for decades. With growing enrollment and limited space, the school is now preparing for its next chapter, a brand-new campus in Prairieland Village,
opening in 2025. Serving students from kindergarten through 12th grade, Cypress Christian is known for strong academics, a biblical foundation, and standout programs in fine arts and athletics. The new 130,000-square-foot campus is designed to grow with the community. It will accommodate more than 1,000 students and 100 staff members and will feature athletic fields, indoor training facilities, a fine arts wing, and classrooms filled with natural light. For families seeking faith-based education close to home, this move offers a significant new option within Bridgeland.

### Public Schools That Set the Standard
Bridgeland is already home to some of the top-rated public schools in Texas. [Cy-Fair ISD](/northwest-houston-schools-real-estate/cypress-fairbanks-isd-real-estate/) anchors the area, with Bridgeland High School, Pope Elementary, and Wells Elementary all earning recognition from the Texas
Education Agency. A third elementary school, McGown Elementary, also serves the community, offering more options as Bridgeland continues to grow. To keep pace with demand, a new middle school is currently under construction, and an additional elementary campus is planned for Parkland Village.

### Harmony Public Schools Join the Mix
Choice in education is important, and Bridgeland is about to welcome its first charter school, thanks to Harmony Public Schools. Known for their focus on STEM and college prep, Harmony has acquired land at the intersection of
Grand Parkway and Creekland Village Drive. The first phase of Harmony School of Bridgeland will open ahead of the
2025-2026 school year, initially serving grades pre-K through second and sixth. Middle and high school buildings will follow. Harmony’s track record is impressive. With a 100% college acceptance rate and national recognition from outlets like U.S. News & World Report and Newsweek, this addition adds even more strength to Bridgeland’s already
robust academic offerings.

### Early Learning Starts Strong
For younger children, The Goddard School of Cypress (Bridgeland) provides a nurturing space filled with play-based learning and early development. With a focus on social, emotional, and academic growth, it’s a popular choice for
families looking to get their little ones off to a strong start.

### A Smart Move for Families
Bridgeland’s commitment to educational excellence continues to make it one of the most family-focused communities in the Cypress area. Whether you’re seeking top-tier public schools, private faith-based instruction, or innovative charter programs, the options here are expanding to meet the needs of the growing population.
