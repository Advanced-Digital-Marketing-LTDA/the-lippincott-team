      answer: |
        Cypress's single-family median active listing price was [$450,000 in September 2026](https://www.har.com/cypress/realestate/pricetrends) (HAR.com). This is an asking-price median, not a sale-price median. Your value depends on subdivision, condition and recent comparable sales. Request a free data-backed valuation.
    - question: How long does it take to sell a home in Cypress?
      answer: |
        Cypress single-family homes sold in August 2026 had a [median of 31 days on market](https://www.har.com/cypress/realestate/pricetrends) (HAR.com). Active Cypress single-family listings had a 42-day median age in September 2026 (HAR.com), which measures how long current listings have been on the market, not how long a sale takes. Greater Houston single-family sales averaged [54 days in August 2026](https://www.har.com/content/department/mls?m=09&y=2026). Accurately priced, well-presented homes sell fastest.
